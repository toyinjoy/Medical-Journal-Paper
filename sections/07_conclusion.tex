\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Class imbalance and class overlap are not interchangeable problems. In the dataset-specific factorial experiment, explicit separability pressure changed Vigitel-obesity utility but not COVID-19 utility. In the common augmentation benchmark, the corrected CTGAN comparison supported an AUPRC gain only for Vigitel smoking; the other CTGAN and all TVAE intervals included zero. Simple rebalancing was often competitive, and closer positive-class coverage was accompanied by reduced diversity.

SepAware is an auditable mechanism for controlling and studying class-conditional geometry, not a universally superior augmentation method. Its use requires evidence that a predictive gain survives simple baselines and does not result from removing difficult but legitimate positive cases. Internal repeated cross-validation cannot establish clinical benefit or transportability.
