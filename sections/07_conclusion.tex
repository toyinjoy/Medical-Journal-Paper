\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Synthetic-data utility depends on more than the generator. In a matched internal lock, separability-aware Pareto curation improved minority classification for two Vigitel outcomes by selecting decision-useful CTGAN prototypes and pairing them with a synthetic-training-only threshold. The same claim was not supported for COVID-19 or kidney mortality. Curation consistently narrowed positive-class support and worsened calibration, and longer generator training reduced some advantages.

The Generate--Curate--Decide framework makes these boundaries explicit: candidate-pool adequacy limits what can be selected, curation determines which modes survive, and decision transfer determines whether synthetic ranking information becomes useful real-data classifications. A curation gain is credible only when it survives matched-pool comparison and is acceptable in coverage, diversity, calibration, and intended use.
