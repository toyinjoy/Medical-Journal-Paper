\section{Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:limitations}

This is an internal locked study, not external validation. The datasets had been explored in preceding research, although the final candidate and inference plan did not use the locked outcomes. The two confirmed tasks share Vigitel measurement and population structure, so their agreement cannot be counted as independent source replication. Temporal or cross-site validation is the most important next test.

Inference is conditional on nine fitted model pairs per task. Prediction-swap tests and feature-group bootstraps quantify uncertainty in the locked predictions but do not include every source of training-sample or generator uncertainty. Feature-profile grouping prevents exact-profile leakage but is not a verified patient identifier for all sources. The small COVID-19 lock yields wide uncertainty, and the kidney positive count remains low.

CTGAN was fixed before confirmation because it was the strongest development generator for this research question. The study therefore evaluates curation within CTGAN and cannot establish transfer to other generator families. The epoch analysis also shows that a stronger Standard CTGAN can narrow the observed advantage. A compute-matched comparison of longer training versus larger-pool curation would be required for an efficiency claim.

Profile compatibility uses empirical class relations rather than validated causal or clinical constraints. Several smoking variables have uncertain semantics, and distance definitions for mixed and near-constant variables remain approximate. Coverage and diversity are geometric proxies, not clinician-validated phenotype measures. Blinded clinical review and prespecified subgroup or phenotype coverage are needed to determine whether discarded positive cases are redundant, noisy, or clinically meaningful.

Thresholds were optimized for minority-F1 on synthetic training predictions. They are not clinical operating points, and calibration worsened. Any risk-prediction use would require an independent real validation set for calibration and decision-curve analysis. The privacy analysis is likewise insufficient for release: exact matches and nearest-neighbor diagnostics do not replace membership, attribute, or linkage attacks, nor do they provide a differential-privacy guarantee.

Future work should prioritize one independent temporal or cross-site cohort, clinician-defined positive-class coverage, real-validation calibration, and explicit privacy attacks. For kidney mortality, a zero-inflated count-aware or longitudinal generator should be evaluated before additional selector tuning.
