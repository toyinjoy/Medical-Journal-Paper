\section{Supplementary Methods and Results}
\label{app:audit}

\subsection{Evidence hierarchy}

The paper's confirmatory evidence is the locked CTGAN-only TSTR comparison. Earlier repeated-cross-validation augmentation, factorial, generator-development, and architecture-screening studies are development history and are not pooled with the lock. Historical raw outputs remain in the repository audit trail, but comparisons of generator families are outside the scope of this paper.

\subsection{Robust scaling and Pareto ordering}

For a score vector $z$, the robust transformation uses center $\operatorname{median}(z)$ and spread $1.4826\operatorname{median}|z-\operatorname{median}(z)|$. If the spread is degenerate, the interquartile range divided by 1.349 is used; a second degeneracy maps all values to 0.5. Robust $z$ values are clipped to $[-3,3]$ and mapped to $[0,1]$.

The final selector discretizes realism, separability, and profile compatibility to 100 bins per axis. An occupied grid cell dominates another when it is no worse on every objective and better on at least one. Candidates are ordered by the number of occupied dominating cells, then by descending objective values and stable input order. This is a deterministic epsilon-Pareto approximation rather than exact continuous nondominated sorting.

\subsection{Threshold policy}

Each classifier generates grouped three-fold out-of-fold probabilities on the selected synthetic training set. Candidate thresholds are the 1st--99th probability quantiles and 0.5. Thresholds predicting positives at less than one half or more than twice the training prevalence are excluded. Minority-F1, balanced accuracy, and proximity to 0.5 define the lexicographic choice. If no threshold is eligible, the procedure falls back to 0.5.

\subsection{Locked inference}

The primary statistic is the mean minority-F1 difference over three generator seeds and three classifiers. A joint prediction-swap permutation independently exchanges the paired Standard and \sepa{} predictions within fitted-model pair and recomputes the statistic. Feature-group bootstrap resampling preserves exact-profile groups. Both procedures are conditional on the fitted models. Holm correction covers the four task-level primary tests.

\subsection{Mechanism and sensitivity evidence}

The progressive replacement study uses the same saved pool and exact candidate identifiers. It moves from Standard-selected toward \sepa{}-selected rows while preserving class quotas, then refits the downstream classifier. Candidate-pool coverage, final-set overlap, distributional diagnostics, and prediction changes are stored separately so overlap is not treated as a utility metric.

The epoch study contains 240 development CTGAN cells (4 tasks $\times$ 4 epoch budgets $\times$ 5 folds $\times$ 3 generator seeds) plus 48 retrospective locked-sensitivity cells. The development analysis is primary for budget sensitivity; the previously used lock is descriptive in this stage.

\subsection{Excluded claims}

The study does not claim external validation, generator-family superiority, calibrated clinical risk, causal preservation, formal privacy, or safe public release. Earlier multi-generator outputs are retained for auditability but do not contribute to the title, abstract, tables, inference, or conclusions of this manuscript.
