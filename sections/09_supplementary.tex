\section{Supplementary Methods and Results}
\label{app:audit}

\subsection{Output hierarchy}

Fold-level augmentation results are stored in \path{results/raw/predictive_metrics_long.csv}; TSTR results are stored in \path{results/raw/tstr_fold_metrics.csv}. Diagnostic files use the same task, repetition, fold, and condition keys. Aggregate tables are generated from these files and are not edited manually. The experiment manifest records source hashes, features, exclusions, cohort construction, seeds, generator settings, metrics, and task-specific applicability.

The common benchmark contains real-only training, random over- and undersampling, SMOTE, class weighting, standard and selected CTGAN, and standard and selected TVAE in augmentation. TSTR contains the four generator conditions. The full metric matrix includes AUPRC, AUROC, Macro-F1, positive-class F1, precision, recall, balanced accuracy, Brier score, calibration intercept, and calibration slope. Machine-readable summaries are in \path{results/tables/augmentation_metric_summary.csv} and \path{results/tables/tstr_metric_summary.csv}.

\subsection{Repeated-cross-validation inference}

For each task and generator, SepAware and standard observations are paired by repetition, fold, and classifier. Classifier differences are averaged within a fold before inference. If $\bar d$ and $s_d^2$ are the mean and variance of the 15 fold summaries, the corrected standard error is
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{SE}_{\mathrm{NB}}=\sqrt{\left(\frac{1}{15}+\frac{n_{test}}{n_{train}}\right)s_d^2},
\end{equation}
with $n_{test}/n_{train}=0.25$ for five-fold cross-validation. Two-sided $t$ intervals use 14 degrees of freedom. Holm adjustment is applied across the eight prespecified task--generator contrasts. This correction addresses overlap approximately; it does not make internal folds equivalent to independent cohorts.

\subsection{Fidelity and disclosure-risk diagnostics}

Numerical marginal similarity is one minus the Kolmogorov--Smirnov distance; categorical similarity is one minus total-variation distance. Marginal similarity averages these feature-level values. Correlation similarity is one minus half the mean absolute difference between real and synthetic correlation matrices over the imputed numeric and numeric-coded categorical fields. Detection AUROC comes from a classifier distinguishing real training records from synthetic records; 0.5 indicates chance discrimination and values farther from 0.5 indicate easier detection.

Coverage is the mean distance from each held-out positive record to its nearest synthetic positive record. Diversity is the mean pairwise distance among at most 1,000 synthetic positive records. Exact-match rate is the synthetic fraction at zero encoded distance from a real training record. The membership proxy classifies real training versus held-out records using nearest-synthetic distance. None of these metrics provides a formal privacy guarantee.

\subsection{Dataset-specific analyses}

The COVID-19 DACPF files are stored in \texttt{results/dataset\_specific/}. The factorial fold responses, effects, assumption diagnostics, and correlation analyses are stored in \texttt{results/factorial/}. These analyses are not averaged with common-benchmark results. No clinical-anchor experiment was created for smoking or kidney mortality because independently justified anchor definitions were unavailable.

\subsection{Excluded and superseded evidence}

Single-split development notebooks, failed TabPFN runs, and protocols without successful outputs are excluded from numerical claims. The reporting-fold fixed-$\lambda$ search is retained as exploratory development evidence but is superseded for the common benchmark by the fixed manifest setting. The repository audit and task-by-experiment matrix give the status and rationale for every identified experiment.
