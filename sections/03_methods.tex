\section{Materials and Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Study design: Generate--Curate--Decide}

The primary study is a matched-pool, internal locked evaluation of synthetic-only training. The generator family, candidate definition, primary endpoint, success rule, and inference plan were frozen before locked outcomes were accessed. Standard CTGAN and \sepa{} used the same fitted generator, pool, class quotas, downstream models, and training-only threshold policy; only row curation differed. Figure~\ref{fig:gcd} shows the conceptual and experimental separation.

\subsection{Outcome tasks}

Four binary tasks from three health-data sources were used (Table~\ref{tab:datasets}). Vigitel obesity and current smoking share one Brazilian telephone-surveillance source and are not independent cohorts \citep{bernal2017}. The COVID-19 task contains de-identified hospital admissions. The kidney task uses a patient-level landmark with predictors constructed before the landmark and recorded mortality within 365 days. The outcomes span different positive-class frequencies and feature structures; only kidney mortality is below 10\% prevalence, so we use \emph{minority outcome} rather than treating every task as rare.

Missing numerical values were replaced by training-set medians; binary values were normalized to $\{0,1\}$. All preprocessing, generator fitting, scoring, curation, threshold selection, and classifier fitting occurred inside the training partition. Generated numerical values were clipped to real-training ranges and binary fields were rounded and clipped. No held-out feature or outcome fitted preprocessing parameters.

\subsection{Partition roles and leakage control}

Each task was partitioned once by five-way stratified group splitting: fold 0 was locked, fold 1 was reserved for confirmation, and folds 2--4 were used for screening. Records with identical feature profiles were kept in the same group to prevent exact-profile leakage. Final locked training combined the screening and confirmation partitions; the locked test contained 67 COVID-19, 817 kidney, 998 Vigitel-obesity, and 1,000 Vigitel-smoking records. Feature-profile groups are leakage safeguards rather than verified patient identifiers. The source datasets had also been explored in earlier research, so the lock is internal and untouched by this study's final selection, not an external validation cohort.

\subsection{Generate: fixed CTGAN candidate pools}

CTGAN was fixed before confirmation and was not a study factor. For each task, three independently seeded CTGAN models were trained for 30 epochs on the real training partition. Each model produced a shuffled class-conditional pool five times larger than the required synthetic quota. The final \tstr{} dataset reproduced the real training partition's class counts. Standard CTGAN retained the first quota-matched records after the fixed pool shuffle. This makes Standard and \sepa{} a paired comparison within one realized pool rather than a comparison between independent generator fits.

\subsection{Curate: SepAware Pareto selection}
\label{sec:selection}

Let $\mathcal D_R=\{(x_i,y_i)\}_{i=1}^{n}$ be the real training set and $\mathcal C=\{(\tilde x_j,\tilde y_j)\}_{j=1}^{m}$ the CTGAN pool. A mixed-type distance gives numerical and categorical blocks equal weight; high-cardinality numeric-looking fields are excluded from the categorical block. For each class, up to 128 real training prototypes are sampled with the recorded seed.

For candidate $j$, realism rewards proximity to a same-class prototype,
\begin{equation}
R_j=1-U\!\left(d(\tilde x_j,\mathcal P_{\tilde y_j})\right),
\end{equation}
where $U(\cdot)$ is a robust median/MAD transformation clipped to three robust standard deviations and mapped to $[0,1]$. Separability combines a nearest-prototype margin and class confidence,
\begin{equation}
S_j=\tfrac12 U\!\left(d_{\mathrm{miss},j}-d_{\mathrm{hit},j}\right)
    +\tfrac12 U(P_{\mathrm{cf}}(\tilde y_j\mid\tilde x_j)).
\end{equation}
The confidence term averages predictions from three class-balanced random forests trained on disjoint group folds of the real training set. It reduces direct in-sample confidence inflation but is not a fully nested preprocessing pipeline.

Profile compatibility $A_j$ evaluates prespecified predictor profiles using training-only class information. Binary fields use empirical outcome-compatible probabilities; other fields use exponential distance from the corresponding class mean scaled by its standard deviation. These profiles are descriptive constraints, not causal effects or clinical validity labels.

The three objectives $(R_j,S_j,A_j)$ are discretized to a fixed $100^3$ grid. Candidates are ordered by the number of occupied cells that dominate their cell, with realism, separability, profile compatibility, and stable candidate index breaking ties. This epsilon-Pareto rule avoids reducing the three objectives to one tuned weighted sum.

After quota selection, diversity is the mean pairwise standardized distance in a seeded sample of the selected class. If it is below 80\% of Standard CTGAN diversity, 25\% increments of the Pareto selection are replaced with nonoverlapping Standard-selected rows until the floor is met. This repair is part of the frozen method and means that a final selected set can be a hybrid when pure Pareto curation is too concentrated.

\subsection{Decide: synthetic-training-only thresholds}

Logistic regression, random forest, and CatBoost \citep{prokhorenkova2018} were trained only on the selected synthetic dataset. For each classifier, grouped three-fold out-of-fold predictions on the synthetic training set defined candidate thresholds at the 1st--99th probability quantiles plus 0.5. Eligible thresholds produced a predicted-positive rate between one half and twice the synthetic training prevalence. The threshold maximizing minority-F1 was chosen, with balanced accuracy and proximity to 0.5 as tie-breakers. The classifier was then refitted on all synthetic training records and evaluated once on the locked real set.

Minority-F1 was the prespecified primary endpoint. Secondary endpoints were Macro-F1, minority precision and recall, balanced accuracy, AUPRC, Brier score, and expected calibration error (ECE; 10 equal-width bins). AUPRC measures ranking and is threshold independent; minority-F1 measures classification at the learned operating point. The learned threshold is not a clinically validated decision threshold.

\subsection{Inference and success rule}

Each task produced nine paired fitted-model comparisons (3 generator seeds $\times$ 3 classifiers). The primary test was a two-sided paired prediction-swap permutation test with 10,000 permutations, conditional on those fitted model pairs. Confidence intervals used 5,000 feature-group bootstrap replicates of the locked predictions. Four task-level minority-F1 tests were adjusted by Holm's method.

The operating regime was declared confirmed only if both Vigitel tasks had positive minority-F1 and Macro-F1 differences, at least 6 of 9 paired wins, and Holm-adjusted $p<.05$. COVID-19 and kidney mortality were prespecified boundary-condition tests rather than required wins. This rule was frozen before locked-label access.

\subsection{Mechanism and robustness analyses}

Three supporting analyses were kept distinct from locked confirmation. First, a progressive replacement intervention used one shared pool and replaced Standard-selected records with \sepa{} records while holding quotas and downstream evaluation fixed. Second, saved five-fold pools measured local $k$-nearest-neighbor precision, recall, rare-mode coverage, feature-profile duplication, and dataset-specific distribution errors. Third, a prespecified epoch study trained CTGAN at 30, 100, 300, and 1,024 epochs across five folds and three generator seeds to test whether curation gains persisted as Standard CTGAN received more training. These analyses explain mechanisms and sensitivity; they are not additional independent confirmations.

\subsection{Ethics and reproducibility}

The study uses secondary survey and de-identified institutional data. Ethics committee names, protocol identifiers, consent or waiver determinations, and legal access wording must be verified by the authors before submission. Source records are not distributed. The repository records schemas, hashes, seeds, protocols, compact results, and manuscript-generation code; no result in the paper is based on a patient-level source file committed to Git.
